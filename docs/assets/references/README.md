# Reference material policy

Reference images of real products (the PSP's XMB, HOME screen, Saved Data Utility, other
plugins) are **not stored in this repository**: Sony's UI and most third-party screenshots are
copyrighted or of unclear license ([ADR-013](../../architecture/decisions.md#adr-013--sony-assets-are-read-at-runtime-never-redistributed)).

- The curated list of references, with source links, what to learn and what not to copy, is
  [docs/ui/references.md](../../ui/references.md).
- Captures from the owner's PSP made for side-by-side comparison are kept outside the repository
  (e.g. on the PC next to the device logs that are not committed) and referenced by date and
  screen in the research notes.
- An image may be added here only if its license allows redistribution (e.g. CC BY, CC0, or our
  own drawing); record the source URL, author, license and access date next to it.

Nothing is stored here yet.
